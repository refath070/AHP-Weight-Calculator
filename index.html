Method used

Individual judgments are aggregated by geometric mean. Criterion priorities are calculated with the row geometric mean method. Consistency is evaluated using λmax, Consistency Index (CI), Random Index (RI), and Consistency Ratio (CR).

Complete Saaty Pairwise Comparison Scale

Interpret every value available in the judgment dropdowns. The scale always compares Criterion A against Criterion B.

1/9 → 1/2 Criterion B is more important

The smaller the reciprocal value, the stronger the preference for B over A.

1 Equal importance

A and B contribute equally to the objective.

2 → 9 Criterion A is more important

The larger the value, the stronger the preference for A over B.
