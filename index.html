Method used

Individual judgments are aggregated by geometric mean. Criterion priorities are calculated with the row geometric mean method. Consistency is evaluated using λmax, Consistency Index (CI), Random Index (RI), and Consistency Ratio (CR). The calculator includes extended RI values for matrices up to 25 criteria.

Geometric standard deviation (GSD) is calculated in log-ratio space. As a practical screening guide in this tool: GSD ≤ 1.25 = low disagreement, 1.25–1.50 = moderate, and > 1.50 = high. Treat these bands as diagnostics rather than universal cutoffs.

Rank Stability for All Criteria

±10% perturbation

How this sensitivity test works

The selected criterion weight is reduced and increased by the chosen percentage. All other weights are proportionally rescaled so the total remains 1. The resulting ranks are compared with the original ranking. This tests robustness of the weight structure; validation against observed hazard outcomes should still be performed separately.
